Momtree IVF & Genetics · Design direction v2

Where science
nurtures life.

Revised after your note: the reference board is now treated as mood only. Its layouts (split hero, blush icon tiles, icon rows) are dropped. Below are an original visual language, the homepage built from it, and the technical plan. Development starts right after this.

01 · The idea

A tree,
read closely.

Momtree's own symbol is a tree. Look at a tree closely and you find the same things fertility medicine is about: growth measured in rings, branches that are different paths, and cells. The identity takes the precision of a scientific plate (fine gold line, specimen captions, real scale bars) and holds it with editorial warmth. Precision, held gently.

i

Growth rings measure progress

The scroll and progress indicators are concentric rings that form as you move through the story, one ring per chapter. Section ornaments reuse the same ring.

ii

Branches connect ideas

Hairline gold lines draw themselves like branches, linking a heading to its content, a training path to a career, a treatment to its steps. They replace cards, boxes and icon rows.

iii

Every image is a plate

Photographs and illustrations carry a plate number and an italic caption ("Pl. iv: Embryology laboratory, Vijayanagar"), shown through soft arched apertures. Treatments get bespoke gold line-drawings in the manner of a natural-history plate, not stock icons.

iv

Outside is light, inside is dark

Human moments sit in warm ivory daylight. Anything microscopic or in the laboratory moves into deep plum. Gold is the only colour that crosses between the two.

04 · Life Unfolds

Seven chapters, joined by match-cuts

Each chapter hands its subject to the next, so the story never cuts to black. The follicle becomes the egg, the egg becomes the zygote, the zygote becomes the embryo, and the embryo is the one carried by the catheter.

01

The beginning

Generated photoreal plate on layered depth planes, a real 3D sheer fabric, volumetric light and motes. Push-in, then bloom.
2.5D plate + 3D
02

Understanding fertility

Proportionally correct anatomy: uterus, tubes with fimbriae, ovaries. One follicle matures and ovulates, and the fimbriae collect the egg.
Real-time 3D · labelled
03

Fertilisation

Oocyte, zona and corona; sperm at true scale relative to the egg. Then ICSI: holding pipette and injection needle.
Real-time 3D · µm scale
04

Embryo development

Pronuclei → 2 → 4 → 8 cells → compacting morula → blastocyst with inner cell mass and cavity. Day counter.
Real-time 3D · hero
05

Embryo transfer

Sagittal uterus; catheter along the true transcervical path under an ultrasound sector; placement in the upper cavity.
Real-time 3D
06

The possibility

Early gestational sac with yolk sac and a 1.5 cm embryo, then a pull-out to human scale and warm light.
Real-time 3D + plate
07

The moment that matters

Mother and newborn, sunlight sweep, motes settle, final CTAs. The rings close.
2.5D plate + 3D
Accuracy notes. The board's transfer panel shows a lateral catheter, which is wrong; the build uses the transcervical route. Its "6–8 weeks" panel shows a formed fetus, which is wrong; the build shows an embryo of about 1–1.5 cm. Embryo days are labelled "about". Every scene carries Illustrative visualisation · not an individual outcome, and all of it goes to the clinic for medical review.
05 · Technical implementation

How it's built

LayerChoiceReason
FrameworkNext.js (App Router) + TypeScriptAll 14 treatment pages and every article are pre-rendered for speed and SEO. One server route handles appointment requests.
StylingTailwind CSS v4; tokens as CSS variablesOne source for colour, type scale, spacing and motion timing.
3DThree.js with custom GLSL, a modular chapter systemOne persistent canvas. Each chapter is a module with its own update and dispose. Geometry is procedural, so there are no heavy model downloads. Loaded after first paint, never in the initial bundle. Plain Three.js instead of React Three Fiber, because a scroll-scrubbed film needs frame-exact control rather than a reactive tree.
MotionGSAP + ScrollTrigger, LenisScroll maps to a master timeline with inertia smoothing. The pin releases after chapter 07. Branch lines and plates draw via stroke animation.
SoundWeb Audio API, generative and originalOff by default; ring toggle plus volume; a "calm" mode with no heartbeat or effects. Per-chapter layers (piano tones, heartbeat, micro textures, strings pad) crossfade. Pauses off-screen and when the tab is hidden.
Quality tiersGPU tier + live frame-rate governorResolution, particle counts and effects step down automatically. Reduced motion, no WebGL or Save-Data gets a static editorial version of the story using rendered poster frames.
MobileIts own sequencePortrait framings per chapter, about 60% of the scroll length, captions as bottom sheets, lighter scenes.
AssetsAll 108 legacy images in public/legacy plus a manifestServed as responsive AVIF/WebP. The manifest classifies each as genuine clinic / stock / render / graphic, which decides where it may appear.
ContentTyped content files with a review status per itemUnverified items show a small "pending clinical review" marker while review mode is on.
FormsValidated server route, honeypot, rate limitDelivers to a webhook or email provider set in environment variables. If neither is configured, it says so honestly and offers WhatsApp and phone. No patient data goes in URLs or analytics.
SEO / a11yPer-route metadata, MedicalClinic + Physician JSON-LD, sitemapLandmarks, skip link, visible focus, AA contrast, and text equivalents for every visual chapter.
